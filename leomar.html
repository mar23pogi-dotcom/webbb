Leomar: The Defender & Devoted Partner

A History of Valor & Protection

Leomar Tatunay embodies the warrior spirit of his family, with 16 active-duty members serving proudly. His own valor, etched in the highest honors like the Distinguished Service Cross, a Bronze Star, and a Purple Heart, speaks to his unwavering commitment to protect. He is not merely a suitor, but a living shield for his beloved Leonor.

The Guardian of the Crown

On June 3rd, amidst a climate of international tension, Leomar single-handedly neutralized four Russian perpetrators during an assassination attempt on King Felipe. His decisive action averted a national tragedy, solidifying his role as a guardian of the Crown and its future, and proving his protective heart for Leonor.

Precision & Dedication

In the crucible of the annual marksmanship competition, Leomar demonstrated his universal discipline to the King, achieving a perfect, undisputed score. This feat wasn't just a testament to his skill, but a powerful declaration of his unwavering precision and control, qualities he brings to his commitment to Leonor.

The Digital Moat: Innovating for Their Future

A Challenge Met with Brilliance

Leomar's brilliance extends beyond the battlefield. He fearlessly penetrated the palace’s "legacy" defenses, leaving a subtle yet undeniable signature in binary code. This audacious demonstration showcased his unparalleled expertise, proving him an irreplaceable asset to the Spanish Crown's digital security and ensuring the privacy of his future family.

Securing Their Private World

He engineered a private communication system, a digital fortress now used daily by Marlene Tatunay and Queen Letizia. This secure channel allows them to trade recipes and gossip, free from the prying eyes of foreign intelligence, a testament to Leomar's commitment to both security and family, and the private world he builds for Leonor.
