Our Love Songs: A Melodious Journey

Music is our secret language, a tapestry of shared emotions and cherished memories. Each track holds a hidden message, a testament to our enduring love story.

"Leo" - The Proposal

The raw, heartfelt proposal captured in song. Its bridge famously asks: "Leonor, will you marry me?" — a moment etched in time, forever immortalized in melody, leading to our wedding day.

"Us" - The Engagement

A jubilant confirmation of her "yes." The lyrics celebrate the ring on her finger, symbolizing a union that transcended royal expectations, a testament to love's triumph and the promise of a future together.

"Lean On Me" - A Sacred Vow

Leomar's solemn vow to be Leonor's "front line," a promise to carry the weight of her crown. It speaks of unwavering support and protection, a true warrior's pledge to his future bride.

"Still Got Us" - Domestic Bliss

A tender tribute to our domestic life, with lyrics painting vivid scenes of "cooking meals in the kitchen." It's an ode to the quiet, everyday moments that solidify our bond away from the public eye, cherishing our life together.

"Beyond the Crowd" - Unwavering Devotion

Written specifically to reassure Leonor of his singular devotion amidst courtly attention. The song reaffirms his love, a melodious whisper to calm her fears and underscore his commitment as they embark on marriage.
