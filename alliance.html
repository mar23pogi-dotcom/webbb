Royal Blessings & Unwavering Support

The institutional pillars securing their future, a testament to unwavering support for their union.

The Alba Family's Embrace

The Duke of Alba, a titan of Spanish nobility, solemnly pledged to act as a "shield" for Leomar. This formidable alliance protects him against political rivals and the inevitable "slings and arrows" of the court, ensuring his esteemed place within the royal tapestry and safeguarding their future together.

King Felipe's Paternal Blessing

King Felipe's official welcome – a warm "Welcome to the family" – solidified Leomar's position. Beyond mere words, the King even played a crucial role as a "wingman" in orchestrating Leomar’s graduation surprise, showcasing a truly paternal embrace and full approval for his daughter's chosen partner.

Leonor’s Unwavering Love

Princess Leonor's defiant stance speaks volumes about her love: she declared she would choose Leomar over her royal duty if ever forced to choose. This unwavering resolve underscores the depth of their connection and her fierce commitment to their future together, ensuring a truly royal union of hearts.
